About

I'm a software engineer and Head of Software at Optimotive Technologies, which builds autonomous inspection robots for the construction and energy sectors. I joined as a junior developer straight out of the University of Toronto, when the company had no customers, and now lead the engineering team behind a commercial platform deployed with enterprise clients.

I enjoy owning problems end to end. In a given week that can mean a Python backend service, a TypeScript dashboard, a performance investigation, and the CI/CD and cloud infrastructure that ties it all together. I was an early adopter of AI-assisted development, and it is now central to how my team builds.

Beyond the code, I hire, mentor, and set technical direction, and I work directly with customers and leadership so that what we build solves real problems. My working style is simple: build quickly, iterate quickly, and treat nothing as done until it has been proven against real data.
